### Engineering Focus & Stance

### Operational Capabilities

#### 01 / Threat Validation & Resilience Testing

* **Autonomous & Manual Penetration Testing**: Safe, non-disruptive adversary emulation targeting hybrid networks, APIs, and enterprise identity perimeters mapped to MITRE ATT&CK.
* **Detection & SOC Verification**: Empirical validation of live security monitoring pipelines to confirm alerts trigger before lateral movement occurs.
* **The 11-Point Threat Blueprint**: Rapid posture assessments delivering verified findings and actionable remediation snippets instead of static CVSS scorecards.

#### 02 / Defensive Architecture & Hardening

* **Infrastructure Engineering**: Multi-cloud landing zone baselines (AWS, Azure, GCP), identity-first isolation, and Policy-as-Code guardrails.
* **Zero Trust Boundaries**: Hybrid connectivity design, egress inspection, and micro-segmentation controls.
* **Digital Footprint Hardening**: Executive and remote workspace perimeter security, hardware-backed authentication (FIDO2), and credential isolation.

#### 03 / Operations & Technical Advisory

* **Managed Security & IT Operations**: End-to-end technology lifecycle management, user onboarding/offboarding workflows, and endpoint security baselines.
* **Incident Readiness**: DFIR playbooks, forensic readiness engineering, and tabletop exercises.
* **Fractional Leadership**: Strategic vCISO advisory, pre-audit architectural alignment, and tooling evaluation.
